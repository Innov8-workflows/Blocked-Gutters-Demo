Deep flow gutters carry more water than a standard half round, which helps on bigger roofs. The photos on this page show new black gutters and fascias over white soffits, on a brick house and on a rendered one with the scaffold tower still up.

Old Boards Out, New Ones In

Timber fascias rot once a gutter has been overflowing onto them for a few winters. On a replacement the old gutter and boards come off, new UPVC boards go up, and the new gutter is hung on them.
