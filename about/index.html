Meet The Owner

The Team Behind Blocked Gutters Ltd

Blocked Gutters Ltd is run by Kieran Manser from Parkview Way in Epsom. We've been on Checkatrade since July 2015, and the team is 4 strong, with over 15 years of experience between us.

We clean gutters and downpipes, take moss off roofs with steam and treat them with biocide, clean UPVC, and fit new UPVC deep flow gutters, fascias and soffits, for homeowners, landlords and businesses.

Why Choose Us

What Makes Us Different

  1. 01

    The Sky Vac, Up to Three Storeys

    We clear gutters with a mix of safe ladder access and the Sky Vac gutter vacuum, whose carbon fibre poles reach up to three storeys, and every clear includes a downpipe check and an air flow test. Gutter cleaning.

  2. 02

    Steam, Then Biocide

    Moss comes off with super heated steam, which isn't abrasive, then the roof is treated with biocide. Roof moss removal comes with a 5 year moss free guarantee. Roof cleaning and moss removal.

  3. 03

    A 10-Year Guarantee on Installations

    New UPVC deep flow gutters, fascias and soffits come with a 10-year guarantee. New gutters, fascias and soffits.

  4. 04

    Fixed Prices, Free Quotes

    Every property is different, so we give fixed prices and free, no-obligation quotes. Ask for yours.

  5. 05

    Insured and on Checkatrade Since 2015

    Our public liability insurance covers up to £5,000,000, and we've been on Checkatrade since July 2015. See our Checkatrade profile.
