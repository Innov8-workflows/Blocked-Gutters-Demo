About Us

The Team Behind Blocked Gutters Ltd

Blocked Gutters Ltd is run by Kieran Manser from Epsom. We specialise in gutter cleaning, roof moss removal and the installation of UPVC fascias, soffits and gutter systems, and we have been on Checkatrade since July 2015.

Roof moss comes off with super-heated steam rather than high-pressure cold water, and the roof is then treated with biodegradable biocides so it stays cleaner for longer. Our uniformed team and sign-written vans are a familiar sight across Surrey.
